# The car

{% image "images/car/front.jpg", "Sunset Riot Caterham 420R, head-on view showing the 7 grille and black bonnet stripes", "How it should look when it's finished (Caterham configurator render)." %}

A Caterham Seven 420R, finished in **Sunset Riot**, a properly loud orange, with black stripes over the bonnet and tail.

It's built to be quick on track. That makes it firm and a bit harsh on the road, but it's fully road legal, so it can be driven to the circuit, lapped all day and driven home again.

## Full specification

As ordered from Caterham in May 2026. The story behind it is in [Specifying the car](/log/specifying-the-car/).

### R-Pack
Sport suspension, lightweight flywheel, limited slip diff, uprated brake master cylinder, rear anti-roll bar, four point harnesses and a whole host of aesthetic niceties.

### Body and paint
* Sunset Riot with matt black triple stripe
* Painted '7' grille
* Carbon fibre front cycle wings
* Carbon fibre indicator pods
* Carbon vinyl boot cover

### Chassis, suspension and brakes
* SV Chassis with lowered floors
* Track suspension pack
* Black trackday rollcage
* 13" Apollo black alloy wheels (6" front & 8" rear)
* Front ventilated discs with quad piston calipers

### Engine and exhaust
* 2.0 Ford Duratec with dry sump
* Track day rear exit exhaust system

### Interior
* 620 style carbon fibre dashboard
* Heated carbon seats with padding and embroidered '7' roundel in orange
* Quick release suede Momo steering wheel
* Gearbox and Handbrake gaiters trimmed in Muirhead fine leather
* Armrests trimmed in Muirhead fine leather
* Carbon leather tunnel top
* Map pocket
* Heater

### Lights and electrics
* High intensity lights with LED daytime running lights
* Sequential shift lights
* Battery master cutoff switch

### Weather protection
* Full windscreen, hood and side screens

### Being added during the build
* Interchangeable carbon fibre aeroscreen
* Carbon fibre spa mirrors, with improved mounts
* VBOX lap timing and data recorder

These are renders of my spec from the Caterham configurator. Real photos will follow as the build goes on.
