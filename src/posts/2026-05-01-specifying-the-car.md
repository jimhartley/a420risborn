To say that a Caterham is small, raw and uncomplicated, there's a bloody long list of options to decide upon!

I would have built a 620 if there was a homebuild option but, of course, there isn't. So a 420 was a logical choice, probably the easiest bit of the 'what to build' decision. I intend to drive half a dozen track days a year and am certainly not going to be going to Starbucks in the Caterham, so, R pack was also a fairly straightforward decision. The R pack adds sport suspension, lightweight flywheel, limited slip diff, uprated brake master cylinder, rear anti-roll bar, four point harnesses and a whole host of aesthetic niceties.

{% image "images/car/rear-three-quarter.jpg", "Sunset Riot Caterham 420R from the rear three-quarter, showing the black rollcage and stripe", "The finished spec, as rendered by the Caterham configurator." %}

So that's the base car chosen... Caterham 420R. Next I spent hours and hours on the Caterham website, configuring the car. There are stacks of options, not all are self explanatory to be fair. What you tend to do is say to yourself, "well that's only an extra £200", and, "well if I have that, this will look odd, so I better have that". Before you know it, the list of options that are not included on my car became very small! But, this is not a cost saving exercise is it. If it was, I would be buying something way more sensible. No, this project is self indulgent, it's something that I have wanted to do for a very long time and now I can. So here is the final spec on the order that was placed with Caterham in May 2026.

* Caterham 7 420 (2.0 Ford Duratec with dry sump)
* R-Pack
* Colour - Sunset Riot with matt black triple stripe
* Painted '7' grille
* Black trackday rollcage
* SV Chassis with lowered floors
* Track suspension pack
* 13" Apollo black alloy wheels (6" front & 8" rear)
* Front ventilated discs with quad piston calipers
* 620 style carbon fibre dashboard
* Gearbox and Handbrake gaiters trimmed in Muirhead fine leather
* Carbon leather tunnel top
* Map pocket
* Heated carbon seats with padding and embroidered '7' roundel in orange
* Quick release suede Momo steering wheel
* Heater
* Sequential shift lights
* Carbon fibre front cycle wings
* High intensity lights with LED daytime running lights
* Carbon fibre indicator pods
* Track day rear exit exhaust system
* Battery master cutoff switch
* Full windscreen, hood and side screens
* Armrests trimmed in Muirhead fine leather
* Carbon vinyl boot cover

{% image "images/car/dashboard.jpg", "Carbon fibre dashboard with Seven 420R logo, gauges, toggle switches and Momo steering wheel", "The 620 style carbon dash and Momo wheel (configurator render)." %}

In addition to the factory supplied kit above, I am looking to have interchangeable carbon fibre aeroscreen, carbon fibre spa mirrors, improved mounts for the mirrors, and, a VBOX lap timing and data recorder. These items will form part of my build rather than being tackled afterwards. My theory being that some things are better addressed sooner, saving headaches further down the road.
