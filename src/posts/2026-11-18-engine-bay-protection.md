title: Engine bay protection
stage: front-end
---
I have read about the grot traps and haven’t ever really seen what they are. When I looked at my car, I could see instantly where the dirt would gather but also that there is a lot of exposed aluminium, which, will pretty quickly form that white furry kind of oxidisation. I decided to paint all of that exposed aluminium in the engine bay with a tough matt black heat-resistant paint. I don’t honestly know if that is the best product for the job but figured that it has to be better than doing nothing, and time will tell.

{% image "images/front-end/masked-1.jpg", "The front of the chassis covered in blue masking tape, with the inner aluminium panels sprayed matt black", "Masked up and painted." %}

{% image "images/front-end/masked-2.jpg", "Another view of the masked engine bay, with blue tape over the chassis tubes and black paint on the side panels", "A couple of rolls of masking tape later." %}

The job was pretty straightforward, a couple of rolls of masking tape and an hour sticking it everywhere I could see that I didn’t want paint. Then, a coarse sandpaper to give the surface a key to help the paint bond, followed by a good clean up with a cloth and brake cleaner. I kept the spray paint in my warm cabinet (definitely worth heating a cabinet with a simple pipe heater) and gave the surfaces five very light coats of spray. I think it looks pretty good. A quick job that hopefully will enhance the overall quality of my car when it’s built.
